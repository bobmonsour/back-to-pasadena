About this home

Tucked within one of Pasadena's most coveted enclaves, this reimagined 1929 Storybook cottage among the four original residences on its block unfolds as a richly layered narrative of heritage and design. Recently showcased on Homeworthy, the home was thoughtfully redesigned in 2023, where every intervention feels both reverent and revelatory. Upon entry, the living room reveals itself with understated drama: a vintage wood mantel crowned with leathered marble frames the fireplace, while built-in bookshelves and a window seat lend both intimacy and function. In the dining room, natural light pours through a charming dormer window, illuminating vaulted wood-beam ceilings and walls dressed in French molding and evocative English wallpaper. The kitchen, however, is the home's undeniable centerpiece. Here, skylights punctuate the continuation of vaulted beams above, while underfoot, repurposed vintage black-and-white marble flooring introduces a graphic elegance. A custom French blue Ilve range anchors the room, complemented by a hand-painted hood, leathered Carrara marble countertops, and custom oak cabinetry adorned with vintage hardware. Sub-Zero integrated refrigerator drawers and a deVOL unlacquered faucet complete the composition, each element chosen with a collector's eye. Beyond, the private quarters continue the home's narrative richness. The primary suite is a study in texture and romance, featuring muraled wallpaper and an antique French marble mantel repurposed as a decorative focal point. The adjoining bath layers pattern and polish with blue-and-white floral marble floors, a Carrara marble shower, a soaking tub by Kohler, and a bidet by TOTO, a thoughtful convergence of beauty and modern comfort. Secondary spaces are equally considered: a guest bedroom opens to the garden through iron casement doors, while upstairs, a third bedroom pairs a built-in twin bed with a generously scaled custom wardrobe. The guest bath, clad in brown-and-black checkered tile and finished with a stone-topped vanity and patterned Spoonflower wallpaper, offers a playful counterpoint to the home's softer palettes. A voluminous den easily envisioned as a fourth bedroom extends the living experience, framed by windows and vaulted ceilings, and opening through French doors to a lush, English-inspired garden. Outdoors, curated greenery and dining spaces evoke a sense of pastoral retreat within the city. Even the former garage has been reimagined, now serving as a finished gym with its own climate control, while offering future potential as an ADU. Recent updates including a new roof, upgraded systems, and refinished original hardwood floors bring modern comfort to the home, while vintage light fixtures add character throughout. Positioned near Victory Park and the beloved Pasadena Farmers Market, and just moments from the cultural expanse of The Huntington Library, Art Museum, and Botanical Gardens and the academic prestige of California Institute of Technology, the home offers both retreat and connectivity. Access to Washington Boulevard's boutiques and cafes, as well as the 210 freeway and Metro, places the best of Pasadena within effortless reach.

Key things to know

  • Reimagined 1929 Storybook cottage with vaulted beam ceilings and English wallpaper.
  • Central kitchen features skylights, vaulted beams, custom French blue Ilve range, vintage marble.
  • Primary suite with mural wallpaper and antique French marble mantel, blue-white marble bath.
  • Former garage transformed into a climate-controlled gym with ADU potential and garden.
  • Near Victory Park, Farmers Market, Huntington Library, and Cal Tech.

A magical place awaits. Welcome to the Storybook Tudor architecture of 2089 E. Mountain Street. Built in 1929 and one of the four original homes on the block, this delightful home has only had three owners in its 90-year lifespan. The first time on the market in over 50 years, the original front door with original hardware opens into the warm and inviting living room with original brick fireplace with the original adjacent wood box. The red oak hardwood floors are also original throughout the house, as are the plaster walls with the gorgeous and original “Spanish Lace” finish. The dining room is bright and light and take special note of the original double-doors leading into a newly remodeled kitchen with newer Viking stove and Subzero refrigerator. New quartzite counters and subway tile make for period appropriate yet modern features that include an eat-in bar. Brand new single basin stainless steel sink, newly painted cabinets with new hardware finish the look. Enjoy three nice-sized bedrooms, all with original glass windows and cedar closets. Both bathrooms have been completely remodeled with quartz counters, reglazed original tub and reglazed shower, and stunning slate floors. Relax in the large and open sunroom overlooking the charming, private, and peaceful backyard. Plenty of off-street parking or small boat/RV behind gates. This is a one-of-a-kind home, a rare find in Pasadena, updated and ready for a new family to love it and call it home.
